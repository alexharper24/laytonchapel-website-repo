Ministries / AWANA

AWANA clubs

Wednesday nights at Layton Chapel, from September through May, for children age 3 through high school.

AWANA meets every Wednesday from 6:00 to 7:30 PM, September to May. AWANA provides a dynamic approach for children to learn God's Word, with Scripture memory, games and a Bible lesson every week.

Adult Bible study runs at the same time, so parents can drop children at their club and go straight to their own study.

For parents new to AWANA

What a Wednesday night looks like

AWANA is a long-running children's program built around learning the Bible. A club night is part game time, part Scripture memory and part Bible lesson, and children work through a handbook at their own pace across the year.

You do not need to be a member of Layton Chapel to bring your child, and you do not need to have any church background at all.
