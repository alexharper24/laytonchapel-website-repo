Giving

Supporting the work here

Everything given to Layton Chapel supports the ministry of this church and the work it does in Anderson Creek and Spring Lake.

Three ways

How to give

In person

During any service. Offering is received in the Sunday morning and Sunday evening worship services.

By mail

Layton Chapel Baptist Church
1230 Anderson Creek School Rd.
Spring Lake, NC 28390

Online

Through EasyTithe, the church's online giving provider. One time or recurring.

Giving online opens EasyTithe in its own window, and it handles the payment. Layton Chapel does not see or store your card details.

Give online

You are not expected to give in order to attend

If you are visiting, please do not feel any obligation. Come, sit, and hear the preaching. Nobody will pass you a plate expecting anything.

Where it goes

Gifts support the preaching and teaching ministry, the children's ministries including AWANA, the upkeep of the building, and the outreach the church does in the community.

Check this

Confirm with the church that the EasyTithe link is current, and ask how they want online giving described. Also ask whether they want giving categories named here (general fund, missions, building, AWANA) and whether they issue annual giving statements.

Questions about giving

Ask the church office

For anything about designated gifts, giving statements or estate giving, the church office is the place to start.
