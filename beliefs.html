What We Believe

Our statement of faith

Layton Chapel is an independent Baptist church, and we preach from the King James Bible.

What follows is what Layton Chapel Baptist Church holds and teaches. If you are considering visiting, or thinking about joining, this is the page to read first.

What we can say today

These two points are already published by the church and are not in question.

Bible

We preach from the King James Bible.

Affiliation

We are an independent Baptist church. We are not part of a convention or denominational hierarchy, and the congregation governs itself.

The full statement

Replace this: the statement of faith itself

The church has a complete statement of faith on paper. It has never been published, and the old website told visitors to phone the office for a copy. It needs to be typed here exactly as the church provides it, article by article, with nothing summarized, softened or reworded. Do not draft a substitute.

Suggested markup once the document arrives: an <h3> per article (the Scriptures, God, Jesus Christ, the Holy Spirit, man, salvation, the church, baptism and the Lord's Supper, last things, and so on) with the church's own text beneath each one.
