The Gospel

The message at the center of everything we do

Jesus said, "I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life." John 8:12

Pastoral review required before publishing

Every line of this page needs Pastor Chancey's approval. The Scripture is quoted from the King James Bible and the order follows the way the Gospel is commonly presented, but the wording between the verses is a draft and the church should have the final say on it. Do not publish this page unreviewed.

If you have never been told plainly what the Bible says about God and about you, this page is here for that. It is short on purpose. Every claim on it comes from Scripture, and the verses are quoted so you can read them for yourself.

God made you, and He is holy

The Bible begins with God as Creator, and it describes Him as perfectly holy. Everything He made was good, and He made people to know Him.

Every one of us has sinned

Scripture does not divide the world into good people and bad people. It says the problem reaches all of us.

"For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God."
Romans 3:23

Sin has a penalty

Sin is not a small thing to a holy God, and the Bible is honest about what it costs.

"For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord."
Romans 6:23

Christ paid it Himself

This is the heart of the Gospel. God did not wait for anyone to clean themselves up first.

"But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us."
Romans 5:8

"For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures."
1 Corinthians 15:3–4

It cannot be earned

Salvation is not a reward for good behavior or church attendance. Scripture calls it a gift.

"For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast."
Ephesians 2:8–9

"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life."
John 3:16

What the Bible asks of you

Not a ceremony, not a payment, and not a promise to do better. It asks you to believe and to call on Him.

"That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved."
Romans 10:9

"For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved."
Romans 10:13

"Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house."
Acts 16:31
