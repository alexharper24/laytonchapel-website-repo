Other ways to listen

Sermons anywhere

Our audio is carried by SermonAudio, which puts it on phones, smart speakers, streaming players and even a plain telephone line. Our church code is 33622.

Full audio archive

Layton Chapel on SermonAudio, with downloads and past series.

Phones and tablets

Install the free SermonAudio app on iPhone, Android, iPad or Kindle Fire, then search the ChurchOne section for church code 33622.

TV and streaming players

Available on Fire TV, Roku, Apple TV, Android TV and Chromecast through the SermonAudio app.

Smart speakers

Add the SermonAudio skill to Alexa, then say "Alexa, open SermonAudio" and give church code 33622.

By telephone

No internet needed. Dial (712) 432-3410 and enter church code 33622. Long distance charges may apply.

Better in person

Come and sit with us

Watching online is a good way to start, and it is not the same as being in the room. Morning worship is at 11:00, and you are welcome any Sunday.
